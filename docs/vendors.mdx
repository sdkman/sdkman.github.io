One of the things that makes SDKMAN! special is that vendors publish their own
releases straight onto the platform. The moment you cut a release, it can be a
`sdk install` away for millions of developers, straight from your own CI/CD
pipeline.

Publishing runs through the **SDKMAN! State API**: you authenticate, push a new
version, and choose which one your users get by default.

## Is your SDK a good fit?

SDKMAN! is built for the **JVM ecosystem**, so there are a few different routes
in depending on what you maintain.

### Java distributions

Every Java distribution (Temurin, Corretto, Zulu, GraalVM, and friends) is pulled
in automatically from the [Foojay DISCO API](https://api.foojay.io). There's
nothing for you to publish by hand. Want to add a **new** JDK distribution? Come
and say hello in the **#vendors** channel on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/sdkman) and we'll help you get it onboarded.

### JVM SDKs other than Java

Maintain a build tool, language, framework or CLI in the JVM space? You're in the
right place. You'll get a vendor account and publish your own releases through
the API. The rest of this page is for you.

### Everything else

If your tool has no connection to the JVM universe, such as Go, Rust, Node.js,
Python or Ruby toolchains, then SDKMAN! isn't the right home for it. Not sure
which side of the line you're on? Just ask in
[#vendors](https://discord.gg/sdkman) before applying.

## Requesting a new candidate

If your SDK isn't a candidate on SDKMAN! yet, we'll set it up for you. Email
**[info@sdkman.io](mailto:info@sdkman.io)** with your candidate's name, a short
description and its website, and we'll register it and provision your vendor
account.

:::note

A self-service workflow for candidate and version management is on the way. Until
then, email is the way in.

:::

## Getting started

Ready to publish? The
[Vendor Onboarding guide](https://github.com/sdkman/sdkman-state/wiki/Vendor-Onboarding)
walks you through requesting access, getting provisioned, and pushing your first
release. Or just start a thread in the **#vendors** channel on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/sdkman) and we'll take it from there.
